ABOUT

• A Legacy of Service

The SSF Kariparambu Unit Committee stands as a beacon of Islamic education, social service, and youth empowerment, deeply rooted in the rich tapestry of Kerala cultural and religious heritage. Established in 1973, the committee has been instrumental in shaping the lives of countless students.

• A Unit Steeped in Knowledge and Service

Kariparambu, a unit renowned for its resiliency and progressive spirit, has been a cradle of intellectual and spiritual awakening. The region has been blessed with the presence of luminaries whose contributions have shaped the course of Islamic thought and practice.

• Our Vision and Mission

SSF Kariparamba is committed to creating a society where knowledge, compassion, and unity prevail. Our vision is to empower the youth, instill strong Islamic values, and contribute meaningfully to the betterment of society.

Through our various programs and initiatives, we aim to :

    • Provide quality education and skill development opportunities.

    • Undertake social welfare activities to address community needs.

    • Foster interfaith harmony and promote religious tolerances.

    • Develop leadership qualities in young people.

We invite you to explore our website to learn more about our initiatives and how you can get involved.

• SUPPORT US

It is a youth movement working for the spiritual, cultural, and educational progress of society. Our activities aim to guide students and the community towards the path of goodness.
